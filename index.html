03 — A BRIEF HISTORY

Moments that remain.

From the first human-made impact to the Apollo era, each mission added a chapter to lunar history.

1959

Luna 2

The Soviet spacecraft became the first human-made object to reach the lunar surface by impact.

1966

Surveyor 1

The first U.S. spacecraft to make a successful soft landing on the Moon returned surface images and engineering data.

1969

Apollo 11

Humans walked on the Moon. The Lunar Module descent stage and deployed scientific equipment remained at the site.

1971

Apollo 15

The mission deployed scientific equipment and the first lunar roving vehicle used by astronauts.

1972

Apollo 17

The final crewed Apollo lunar landing left behind equipment and experiments that mark the site.

04 — WHY LUNAR LEGACY

Not abandoned.
Part of the story.

These objects are more than discarded equipment. They are evidence of the challenges humans overcame, the science they pursued, and the future they imagined. By documenting them carefully, we help keep the history of exploration accessible to the next generation.
